# University of Ibadan Postgraduate College — Fee Schedule (Reference Compilation)

Source: Compiled from publicly published University of Ibadan Postgraduate College fee
information (pgcollege.ui.edu.ng/fee) and official fee announcements, current as of the
2026/2027 session. The Postgraduate College's online Fee Checker
(https://pgcollege.ui.edu.ng/setup_fee/) gives the exact, department-specific figure for a
given programme and should always be treated as the authoritative source — figures below are
for general guidance only and may vary by department, nationality, and session.

## 1. Acceptance Fee

- Acceptance Fee for all newly admitted postgraduate students: NGN 30,000.
- Payable once, after an offer of admission, to secure a place on the programme.

## 2. Tuition Fees — Nigerian Students (Full-Time)

| Programme Type | Arts / Social Sciences | Science / Technology |
|---|---|---|
| Postgraduate Diploma (PGD) & Certificates | NGN equivalent of $2,000 | NGN equivalent of $3,000 |
| Academic Master's / PGD | NGN equivalent of $2,000 | NGN equivalent of $3,000 |
| Professional Master's (e.g. MBA, MBF) | NGN equivalent of $5,000 | NGN equivalent of $5,000 |

Illustrative Naira tuition for a standard M.Sc./M.A. programme (per session, first two
semesters): NGN 120,000 – NGN 170,000, with returning students in the third semester paying
approximately half of the initial amount. Specialized/professional programmes can range from
NGN 400,000 to NGN 500,000 per session.

## 3. Tuition Fees — African Students (Non-ECOWAS)

| Programme Type | Arts | Science / Technology |
|---|---|---|
| Diploma / Academic Master's | $2,500 | $4,000 |
| Professional Master's | $5,000 | $5,000 |

## 4. Tuition Fees — International (Foreign) Students

| Programme Type | Arts | Science / Technology |
|---|---|---|
| Diploma / Academic Master's | $3,500 | $5,000 |
| Professional Master's | $6,000 | $6,000 |

Additional charges applicable to foreign students (billed in USD):
- Examination fee (Masters/Diploma): $300
- Postgraduate Development Fee: $400
- Bench fee (laboratory-based programmes): $300

## 5. Other Sundry Charges (Nigerian Students, NGN)

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Academic transcript | 1,000 |
| Application form — Academic programmes | 7,750 |
| Application form — Professional programmes | 10,750 |
| Late registration penalty | 5,000 |

## 6. What Tuition Covers

Tuition fees cover registration, instruction/tuition, supervision, examinations, and
graduation processing, and include membership of the Kenneth Dike Library and the Students'
Union for the duration of the programme.

## 7. Payment Guidance

- Use the Fee Checker at https://pgcollege.ui.edu.ng/setup_fee/ (domestic) or
  https://pgcollege.ui.edu.ng/intl_fee/ (international) to obtain the exact fee for your
  faculty/department and programme level before payment.
- Fees differ by department even within the same faculty; differences are typically in the
  region of NGN 2,000 – 3,000 for similar programmes, but can be significantly larger for
  professional and specialized programmes.
- For enquiries: pgcollegeuiinformationdesk@gmail.com / informationdesk@pgcollege.ui.edu.ng,
  +234 907 1871 740 (9am–4pm).
