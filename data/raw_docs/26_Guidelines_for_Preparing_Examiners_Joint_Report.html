Provost's Speech

I have a great pleasure, on behalf of the Board of the Postgraduate College, University of Ibadan, to welcome you to the premier university in Nigeria. Whether you are a prospective applicant, a current student, an alumnus, a research collaborator or someone simply visiting our website, I am delighted that you are here.


The Postgraduate College is more than a centre for advanced learning; it is a vibrant community of scholars committed to excellence, innovation, integrity and service. For decades, it has nurtured generations of researchers, professionals and leaders across the globe whose contributions continue to shape academia, industry, government and society..


Embarking on postgraduate studies is a significant investment in your future. At the University of Ibadan, you will find a stimulating academic environment where distinguished faculty, cutting-edge research, interdisciplinary collaboration and a rich tradition of scholarship coalesce to help you realise your aspirations. We are committed not only to producing graduates with advanced knowledge but also to developing critical thinkers, creative problem-solvers and globally competitive scholars well equipped to address the challenges of our rapidly changing world.


As you explore our website, you will discover information about our academic programmes, admissions, scholarships, research opportunities, policies and other support services designed to enrich your postgraduate experience. We encourage you to visit this site regularly and take advantage of the opportunities available through the College.


As the flagship of postgraduate education and research in Nigeria, our vision is to consolidate the enduring legacy of academic excellence, while breaking new frontiers in postgraduate education through innovation, international collaboration, digital transformation and impactful research.


Thank you for your interest in the Postgraduate College. I welcome you into our community of scholars and wish you a rewarding journey of discovery, leadership and lifelong learning.


Welcome to the Postgraduate College, where knowledge advances, innovation thrives and transformative leaders are raised.


Professor K. M. Samuel
