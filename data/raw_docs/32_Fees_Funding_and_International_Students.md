# Fees, Funding and International Students

Source: University of Ibadan Postgraduate College website (pgcollege.ui.edu.ng, Fees & Funding and International Students pages), retrieved 2026-11-01. Exact fees for a programme come from the online Fee Checker at https://pgcollege.ui.edu.ng/setup_fee/ (Nigerian students) and https://pgcollege.ui.edu.ng/intl_fee/ (international students).

Fees and Fundings

Find information on postgraduate's tuition fees and studying costs, as well as funding and scholarships available to help you plan your finances.

Fees

Your tuition fees will cover the cost of your postgraduate research programme at the University as well as charges for registration, tuition, supervision, examinations and graduation.

Payment of fees will also entitle you to membership of the Library, and the Students' Union.

Some programmes incur higher expenditure than others and it is, therefore, important that you check the schedule of tuition fees ( Here ) for your chosen programme.

You can find details of individual course fees in the relevant postgraduate programme profiles.

Fundings

The University of Ibadan is committed to supporting postgraduate research students. Each year we invest substantially in doctoral funding.

In addition to these internal funds, the University is highly successful in attracting sponsorship for research students, particularly from the research councils, industries, trusts and charities.

International students

Admissions

We want to make the international admissions process as smooth as possible for students applying to Postgraduate College, the University of Ibadan from outside of Nigeria.

General Requirement

For your application to be successful, you will need to satisfy our general entry requirements and also the requirements of your chosen course.

Many overseas qualifications are acceptable for entry to the University of Ibadan but if you are unsure, you should check against the accepted qualifications listed for your country.

Language Requirement

Language immersion programmes are available for non english speaking candidates/candidates from non english speaking countries.

Visa Guidance

Note: You must have an admission letter & letter of introduction from the postgraduate college.

Application for a visa follows the procedure of the Nigerian mission centre in different countries. Candidates are advised to visit the mission centre for detailed information on visa applications.
